# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Toolkist to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity. We pledge to make participation in this project — through issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other contribution — a respectful experience for all.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in feedback and disagreement.
- Assume good faith; ask questions before assuming bad intent.
- Focus criticism on code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution or close an issue, and that this isn't personal.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, race, religion, or similar characteristics.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and any other communication channel associated with this repository.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening an issue marked clearly as a conduct concern, or by contacting the maintainer directly through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled at the maintainer's discretion, which may include a warning, request to edit/remove content, or removal from the project (blocking, closing PRs, etc.).

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community standards, kept intentionally short for a small single-maintainer project.
